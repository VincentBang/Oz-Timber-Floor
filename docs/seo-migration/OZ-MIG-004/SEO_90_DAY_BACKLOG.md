# OZ-MIG-004 evidence-led 90-day SEO backlog

Rankings are objectives, not guaranteed acceptance tests. Freeze commercial query clusters and owners before intervention; keep branded, non-branded commercial, product/range, local and informational reporting separate.

| Window | Priority | Work | Evidence/guardrail | Measurable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Launch–day 7 | P0 | Validate canonical owners, redirects, sitemap, indexability, forms and approved analytics on the actual domain. | Same immutable release; no URL churn or global noindex removal. | Zero critical routing/indexation/form incidents. |
| Launch–day 30 | P0 | Reconcile GSC indexed pages and landing-page/query movement against matched pre-launch dates. | Use recent complete periods; do not call 12-month averages current rank. | Priority owners discovered/indexable; click/impression loss investigated within one review cycle. |
| Days 1–30 | P0 | Complete Raw Solid Timber evidence and the eight unresolved priority mappings. | Species, grade, dimensions, finish, current status and image identity must be sourced. | Exact keep/control/retire decision for every supplied record. |
| Days 15–45 | P1 | Reconfirm the floor-levelling opportunity and strengthen only gaps shown by current query/page evidence. | Preserve established owner and current practical content; no unsupported standards/tolerances. | Higher qualified levelling enquiries and improved non-branded clicks to its owner. |
| Days 30–60 | P1 | Select 3–5 current near-top-ten commercial clusters across engineered, installation, commercial, hybrid/laminate and supplier intent. | One owner per intent; useful buyer sections and contextual links, no keyword padding. | Improvement in matched landing-page CTR/position plus qualified-enquiry rate. |
| Days 30–60 | P1 | Publish the first verified project records only when evidence and permission exist. | No generic imagery represented as completed work; no invented suburb/client/result. | Assisted enquiries from project pages and reusable proof for relevant service owners. |
| Days 45–75 | P2 | Improve the highest-value verified product/range pages from catalogue evidence. | Reindex only exact factual records with correct local imagery; protect redirect destinations. | More product/range stock or supply enquiries without new quality flags. |
| Days 60–90 | P2 | Earn relevant supplier, trade, industry and local citations/links. | No purchased ranking-manipulation links; outreach/spend needs separate approval. | New relevant referring domains to selected owners. |
| Days 60–90 | P2 | Test buyer-focused homepage/category/installation improvements. | One intervention at a time with baseline, review date and stop/continue rule. | Qualified enquiries, quote rate and won-work contribution improve, not clicks alone. |

For each experiment record: target cluster, owner, evidence window, intervention, protected exclusions, commercial objective, review date and stop/continue rule. Phone/email clicks are intent signals, not confirmed leads.
