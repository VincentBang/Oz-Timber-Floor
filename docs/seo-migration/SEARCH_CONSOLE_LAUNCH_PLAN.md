## Day 1 to Day 3

Monitor:

- Pages report
- Sitemap processing
- Not found / 404
- Page with redirect
- Duplicate without user-selected canonical
- Alternate page with proper canonical
- Crawled currently not indexed
- Server error
- Redirect error

Treat new major 404s, redirect errors, sitemap errors, production noindex or bad canonical reports as urgent.

## Day 7

Review:

- Clicks, impressions, CTR and average position for top pages.
- Old URL impressions moving toward new destination URLs.
- Sitemap discovered URL count.
- New 404 reports.
- Queries where old pages had traffic and new pages are not yet visible.

Do not panic over normal ranking volatility in the first week unless technical issues also appear.

## Day 14 to Day 30

Review:

- Keyword movement for category, service and range pages.
- Pages with high impressions and low CTR.
- Pages in positions 4 to 20 that could benefit from enrichment.
- Redirected old URLs still receiving impressions.
- Any guide/blog pages losing coverage because content is too thin.

Only begin bigger content expansion after the technical migration is stable.
