# When To Hire SEO Help

Date: 2026-06-04

Strategic position: do not hire a generic monthly SEO agency by default. Oz Timber Floor should first run an internal operating system using owner trade knowledge, ChatGPT strategy, Codex implementation, Search Console data and controlled Google Ads learning.

Hire outside help only when the task is specialised, measurable and hard to do internally.

## Good Reasons To Hire Outside Help

| Need | Hire type | Good deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Migration confidence | One-off technical SEO auditor | Crawl report, redirect/canonical/sitemap findings, prioritised fixes |
| Conversion tracking | Google Ads / analytics specialist | Working form, phone, email and campaign tracking with test evidence |
| Google Ads setup | Google Ads specialist | Tight campaign structure, conversion tracking, negatives, search-term review |
| Google Business Profile | Local SEO/GBP specialist | Category/service/photo/Q&A optimisation and citation consistency |
| Backlink outreach | Outreach specialist | Real supplier, builder, partner or local links, not spam |
| Schema/technical issues | Technical SEO specialist | Valid schema, no duplicate/noindex/canonical problems |
| Content editing | Specialist editor | Improve owner-drafted content without losing trade accuracy |

## Bad Reasons To Hire A Generic Retainer

Avoid monthly SEO retainers that only promise:

- vague keyword improvement,
- monthly reports with no implementation,
- low-quality backlinks,
- mass directory submissions,
- generic blog posts,
- AI pages without owner review,
- suburb page spam,
- changes that risk migration stability,
- no clear deliverables or tracked actions.

## Agency Screening Questions

Ask:

1. Which exact pages will you improve this month?
2. Which Search Console queries support that work?
3. What technical issues did you find?
4. What redirects/canonicals/sitemap items will you touch?
5. What backlinks will you build, from where, and why are they legitimate?
6. How will you avoid duplicate pages and keyword cannibalisation?
7. How will you measure enquiries, not just rankings?
8. What work can we see in the repo or site after the month?

If they cannot answer clearly, do not hire them.

## Decision Rule

Use this rule:

- Internal team + ChatGPT/Codex handles strategy, page improvements, internal links, guides and range enrichment.
- Hire specialists for technical audit, tracking, Ads setup, GBP optimisation and legitimate outreach.
- Do not buy a monthly retainer unless deliverables are concrete, page-specific and measurable.

## When To Reconsider An Agency

Reconsider broader outside SEO help if, after 3-6 months:

- migration is stable,
- key pages are improved,
- Search Console shows opportunities but internal capacity is too slow,
- Google Ads proves keywords convert,
- backlinks/proof/outreach become the bottleneck,
- a specialist can show clear deliverables.

## Minimum Contract Terms

If hiring, require:

- page/task list before work starts,
- no redirects/sitemap/canonicals changed without approval,
- no fake reviews or fake projects,
- no unsupported product claims,
- no spam backlinks,
- monthly implementation log,
- Search Console and enquiry impact report,
- ability to cancel if deliverables are weak.
